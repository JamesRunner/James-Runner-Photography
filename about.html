Hi, I’m James.

I’m a photographer and filmmaker based in Riverside, California. I love capturing the world and emulating its beauty, whether that becomes one frame or a whole sequence.

Creating fills me with joy. I’m drawn to warm natural light, honest connection, intentional sound, and places that make people slow down.

My approach is simple. I keep things relaxed and let real moments happen instead of forcing them. I’ll give you direction when you want it and stay out of the way when you don’t.
